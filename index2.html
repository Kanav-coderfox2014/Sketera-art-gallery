The Artist

Pranusha
paints the in-between.

The hour before dawn. The breath between sentences. The warmth of a window left open in October. Pranusha's paintings begin as a feeling she can't name — and end as something you'll recognize.

She works from a small studio in Chandigarh, India, using acrylics and pigments to capture vivid, modern emotions. Each canvas takes 1 month.

"I'm not trying to paint what something looks like. I'm trying to paint what it feels like to remember it."

The Journey

From Studio to Sanctuary

Every painting goes through a careful, slow process before it finds its place on your wall.

01

The Spark

Each piece begins with a feeling—a memory of light, a half-forgotten conversation. Pranusha translates these intangible moments directly onto the canvas.

02

The Craft

Once the painting is dry, it is stretched and framed in solid oak by hand. No mass production, just careful, deliberate craftsmanship that honors the art.

03

The Arrival

Packed in archival materials, your painting arrives ready to hang. A handwritten note from Pranusha accompanies every single piece.
